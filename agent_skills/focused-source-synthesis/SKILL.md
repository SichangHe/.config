---
name: focused-source-synthesis
description: Use when given a focus and asked to synthesize findings from sources, find official sources, compare claims, or provide quote-backed evidence with uncertainty.
---

For searches, always quote the relevant original words verbatim and
provide a pointer to the source.

When summarizing with a focus, use the source instruction:
"What does this paper talk about in terms of FOCUS? Try your best to summarize every single aspect mentioned in the paper. For every single point you elaborate, always quote the exact original related phrase, sentence, or paragraph."

When finding official sources, use the source instruction:
"Do not trust random strangers and blogs on the Internet. Try to find the official sources and quote them."

Use secondary sources only when they add meaningful context.
For web research, search current sources when the fact may have changed.
Keep quotes short and compliant.
Preserve uncertainty, scope limits, population, assumptions, and methods.

Additional answer checks:

- Are you offering what the human specifically wants to know and only that?
- Have you covered all relevant points from all sources?
- Have you clearly linked each claim to its source?
- Have you clearly distinguished facts and possibilities?
- Why, for each point made? Do you provide clear logical reasoning?
- Are you making broad claims when the source only supports a narrow one?
